Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy describes what data the OATH app (Android, package com.oath.app) collects, why, where it goes, how long it is kept and how to delete it.

Simple version: What you write in OATH and the apps you choose to block stay on your phone. An account is optional. If you sign in, we keep your email and plan. Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports go to Google Firebase so we can fix problems. We never sell your data and never show ads.

Who we are

OATH is developed and operated by AlgoLinx Tech, India. Contact: support@algolinx-tech.in.

Accessibility Service

OATH uses Android's Accessibility Service (BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE) for one purpose: to know which app is coming to the screen, so it can show your pause screen when you open an app you chose to block. OATH shows a disclosure screen and asks for your consent before you turn it on.

  • OATH reads only the package name of the app that opens (for example, com.google.android.youtube).
  • It does not read what is on your screen, your messages, passwords or anything you type.
  • Nothing observed through the Accessibility Service is sent off your phone, shared or sold.
  • You can switch it off at any time in Android Settings, Accessibility.

Data that stays on your phone

  • Your vow and the words you write at the pause screen
  • The list of apps you guard, your lock times and limits
  • Your open counts and history shown in Insights
  • Screen time per app, if you allow Usage Access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS). It is only shown to you in the app.

You can erase all of this in the app: Settings, Privacy and data, Delete all data on this phone. Uninstalling OATH also removes it.

Data we collect

Account (optional)

If you create an account or sign in with Google, we store your email address, an account ID, your plan status and the count of unlock attempts (the event type only, never what you wrote). Stored with Supabase (privacy), encrypted in transit.

Purchases

OATH Plus is sold through Google Play Billing. We never see your card or UPI details. RevenueCat (privacy) receives your purchase history and your account ID to confirm your plan on every phone you sign in to.

Usage statistics and crash reports

OATH uses Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics (privacy) to understand which screens are used and to fix crashes. This includes app interactions, device model and Android version, an app instance ID and the Android advertising ID. It is used for analytics only, never for advertising or to build a profile of you. It does not include your vow, what you write, or the apps you block.

Good Cause vote

If you vote for the monthly Good Cause, we store your vote with your account ID.

Other permissions

  • Device admin (optional, Plus): Uninstall protection. OATH holds no device policies; being an active admin only makes uninstalling take extra steps. You can switch it off in OATH or in Android Settings.
  • Notifications: the ongoing notification that keeps blocking running.
  • Ignore battery optimisation: so Android does not stop blocking in the background.

Sharing

We share data only with the service providers named above, only for the purposes above, and when required by law. We do not sell, rent or trade your data, and OATH contains no ads.

Retention

Account and purchase data is kept until you delete your account. Crash reports are kept by Firebase for 90 days; analytics data is kept under Google's retention settings and is not linked to your email.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings, Delete account. This removes your account and the data on our server.
  • Without the app: email support@algolinx-tech.in from your account email with the subject "Delete my OATH account". We delete it within 30 days.
  • On the phone: Settings, Privacy and data, Delete all data on this phone.

A Google Play subscription is not cancelled by deleting your account. Cancel it in Google Play.

Children

OATH is meant for adults (18 and over). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will say so in the app before it takes effect. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Questions or concerns?

If you have questions about this privacy policy or how we handle your data, contact us:

support@algolinx-tech.in

We aim to respond within 48 hours.
